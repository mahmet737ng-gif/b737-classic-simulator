# LE DEVICES — LEDEV-0006

The owner-approved final restored 1984×793 PNG is installed in Unified over the native LE DEVICES plate at x=3, y=540, w=383, h=188 of the frozen 1731×909 OH01–06 source. The locked source pixels and OWNER18 switches are not edited.

26 independently measured lamp apertures: six SLATS (three rows each) and four FLAPS (one TRANSIT and one continuous tall FULL_EXT aperture each). The runtime SVG masks follow the exact dark interiors of the approved PNG and do not redraw its white dividers.

A separate black recessed TEST pushbutton occupies the empty socket. Hold with mouse/touch or Space/Enter: all 26 lamps illuminate; release, cancel, lost capture or blur restores prior device states. The Unified visual lamp test independently forces all lamps on and restores them without latching a physical press.

`panel-owner-positions-v6.json` records the source PNG insertion polygons. `manifest.json` records the installed mapping and SHA-256. `panel.js` preserves the B737LeadingEdgeDevices API, with optional `setGlobalLampTest` and global test state in snapshots. Backend aircraft position inputs remain a separate future integration.

Legacy v5 SVG segments remain archived and are not mounted by v6.
