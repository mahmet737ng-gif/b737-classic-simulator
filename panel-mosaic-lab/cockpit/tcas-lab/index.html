Owner's FCOM SHI: 10.30.12–13, 10.32.29–34, 11.10.27–33, SP.11.1, 15.10.19–26, 15.20.29–32. Owner's MRG Classic: printed pp136, 173, 188, 373.

ATC 1/2 feeds: Electronic Bus 1/2, 115 VAC (MRG CL pp173/188). MRG p373 labels DC feeds for the same equipment; that disagreement remains recorded. The separate TCAS computer feed is not established for this Classic airframe by these sources. The NG transfer-bus feed is not applied here.

ATC2 CB P6-1 D16 agrees with catalogue and Classic index. ATC1 MRG P18-1 B2 conflicts with the catalogue's TCAS label at B2 and ATC1 at B3; TCAS/DME B6 also conflicts. These unresolved CB assignments stay unbound in Unified. The computer and ATC1 failure switches here are explicit instructor inputs.

TCAS interrogates operating transponders independently of ground ATC. A non-transmitting aircraft is invisible; a non-altitude-reporting aircraft cannot generate an RA. Mode C traffic can trigger an RA; coordinated RAs require TCAS on both aircraft (FCOM 15.20.29). TA ONLY may be selected for intentional proximity or limited performance such as an engine inoperative (15.20.31). This lab models received replies and advisory logic; it does not transmit or coordinate RF messages.

TA/RA sensitivity depends on altitude (nominal SL5 TA 40 s / RA 25 s). The simplified trainer predicts vertical separation during horizontal proximity rather than using present separation as a future collision. Converging automatically crosses the own altitude near CPA; the intruder side is configurable under Advanced Settings. Geometric margins, response dynamics and TAS-to-pitch conversion are approximate training parameters. CLEAR OF CONFLICT follows measured/predicted separation; no fixed countdown clears a live threat. This is an encounter trainer, not a complete certified collision-avoidance algorithm. DISPLAY TYPES and SELF TEST intentionally present test symbols.
