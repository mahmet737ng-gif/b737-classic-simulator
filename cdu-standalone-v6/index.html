Operating Guide

Open RTE; use LSK L1 for departure, LSK R1 for destination, TO (R4) for the first route fix, then ACTIVATE (R6) and EXEC.

Press CLR to erase a character or hold it to clear the scratchpad. DIM BRT adjusts training brightness.

This trainer simulates input and pages, not operational navigation or performance.
