Implementation notes

All panel graphics are created with HTML/CSS, with no photo background. Press a physical lever to move it, lift a red guard before changing a protected lever, drag the meter selector, or press the lamp-test button. Buttons and changes are animated. The electrical logic and precise Boeing switch positions remain provisional until cross-checked against the supplied FCOM / detailed panel references.
