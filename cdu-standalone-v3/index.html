Operating Guide

Open RTE, enter the departure airport with the CDU keypad and LSK L1, then destination with LSK R1. Press EXEC when its lamp illuminates.

Press CLR to delete one character; hold to clear the scratchpad. DIM BRT adjusts display brightness.

This is a training input/page simulator, not an operational navigation or performance computer.
