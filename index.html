Using the Cockpit Explorer

Choose a panel or click OPEN next to one. Tap one of the outlined switch targets; use the right-side inspector to change its demo state. Controls are mapped directly on the provided cockpit photograph.

Shortcuts: / focuses search, + and − change zoom, 0 fits, Esc closes dialog. Use the navigation tabs to explore the full flight deck.

Current scope: interaction and persistent demonstration states. The electrical exercise maps selected switch positions from FCOM SP.6.1–SP.6.3 (source PDF pages 196–198). It omits aircraft conditions, annunciation verification, interlocks, failures and the remaining checklist. None of the demo states is suitable for operating an aircraft.
