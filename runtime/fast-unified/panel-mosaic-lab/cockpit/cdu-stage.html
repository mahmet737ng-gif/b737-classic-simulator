Operating Guide

Click RTE enter the four-letter airport identifier using the CDU keypad and LSK L1 then enter the destination in LSK R1 and the first route fix in TO (R4) then select ACTIVATE> (R6) and EXEC to confirm.

Pressing CLR deletes one character; holding it clears the scratchpad. DIM BRT adjusts display brightness in training mode.

This prototype simulates input and page behavior, not live navigation, performance calculation or real aircraft routing.
